# Revision History

**Document status**: Living index. A row is added in the same change as the work
it records, in accordance with `PROJECT_GUIDELINES.md`, Section 2.

**Where this sits**: [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md) says what is being built and what state
each phase is in; [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md) says what the system does today; this
document says how it got there.

## The form of a row

This table is an **index**, not an account. One row per change, in reverse order
of commit, saying what changed and pointing at the two places that hold the
detail: the commit, which records the change as it was made, and the design
document, which records the reasoning and is revised as the design is.

Rows are kept to about sixty words. They were not always: by sub-task 5.8 a
single row had reached 1,564 words in one table cell, restating a design document
that already existed and a commit message that already said the same thing, and
`PLAN.md` had become 17,000 words of which the roadmap was 2,000. Three copies of
an argument do not agree with each other for long.

The rows below sub-task 5.8 predate that rule and are longer than it allows. They
are left as they stand rather than rewritten, a history being a poor thing to
edit after the fact.

| 2026-09-06 | Phase 6 | Sub-task 6.7: the system-call entry path, the dispatch table and the validation of a caller's arguments.
